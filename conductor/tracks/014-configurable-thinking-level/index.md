**Summary**: Real, measured finding from a consuming app (coachai): `gemini-3.5-flash` defaults to `thinking_level: medium` (on), consuming hundreds to thousands of internal "thinking" tokens per call. Measured on a real, representative prompt (~50k chars) with the app's actual call shape (no `max_tokens` cap): default thinking averaged ~27s across 4 trials (2,827-3,633 thinking tokens); `thinkingConfig.thinkingLevel: 'MINIMAL'` averaged ~12s across 4 trials (45-70% faster every single trial, no overlap) with **no observed quality loss** — comparable output length, correct domain-term usage with mechanism explanations, and 100% marker compliance (`<<<DIAGRAM_START/END>>>`, `<<<TERMS_USED/END>>>`) intact across every trial. `@google/genai`'s `thinkingBudget` (token-count-based) was tried first and found NOT to reliably cap thinking for this model (a requested 512-token budget still produced ~768 actual thinking tokens) — `thinkingLevel` (a qualitative MINIMAL/LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH control) is the correct, model-respected lever. Fix: thread a configurable `thinkingConfig`/`thinkingLevel` through `getVertex()`/`getGemini()` as an engine-level default (mirroring Track 013's `llmRequestTimeoutMs` pattern) so a consumer can set it once and have it apply to every call, rather than per-call plumbing.
